What Is Scamming?

Scamming is when a person tricks someone to get their money, personal information or something valuable. Scammers often lie or pretend to be someone they are not so that people will trust them. Scamming can happen on websites, social media, emails and text messages. It can happen to anyone, which is why it is important to be careful when using the internet.


Who Are Scammers?

Scammers are people who try to trick others for their own benefit. They may pretend to be workers from a bank, shop or other company. They do this to make people believe that they are trustworthy. Some scammers send fake messages, make false promises or create websites that look real. Their goal is to make people give them money or private information.


Where Does Scamming Happen?

Scamming can happen on social media, online shopping websites, emails and messaging apps. Some scammers also contact people through phone calls or text messages. This means that people should be careful whenever they communicate with strangers or use websites they do not know.
