WHY SCAMMING IS BAD

Home | Definition | Why It's Bad | Examples | How to Stop Quiz

People Can Lose Money

Scammers trick people into sending them money by making false promises or pretending to offer prizes. Some people believe these messages because they look real. When people send their money, the scammer may disappear without giving them anything. This can cause people to lose money they worked hard to earn.


Private Information Can Be Stolen

Some scammers try to steal private information such as passwords, bank details and phone numbers. They may pretend to be trusted companies to make people share their details. If scammers get this information, they may use it without permission. This can put people's online accounts and personal information at risk.


It Can Make People Feel Upset

Being scammed can make people feel sad, worried or angry. They may feel upset because someone they trusted tricked them. Losing money or private information can also cause stress. This can affect how people feel and make them less confident when using the internet.


People Can Lose Trust

Scamming can make people stop trusting messages and websites. After being tricked, they may become worried about using online services. They may also find it harder to trust people they meet online. This makes using the internet more difficult for them.


It Can Cause Problems for Families

When someone loses money through a scam, it can affect their whole family. The money may have been saved for food, school fees, clothes or other important things. Families may have to change their plans because of the money they lost. This is one reason why scamming is harmful.


It Can Affect Online Accounts

Some scammers try to get into people's online accounts by stealing their passwords. They may use these accounts without the owner's permission. This can cause problems because people may lose access to their accounts or have their information shared with others.
