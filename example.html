Fake Prize Scams

Fake prize scams happen when someone tells a person that they have won a prize, even though they did not enter a competition. The scammer may ask them to send money before receiving the prize. Some people believe the message because it sounds exciting. However, the prize is usually not real and the person may lose money.


Shopping Scams

Shopping scams happen when people create fake online shops to trick customers. They may show pictures of phones, clothes or shoes at very low prices. After someone pays, the product may never arrive. The seller may also stop replying to messages after receiving the money.


Fake Bank Messages

Some scammers send messages pretending to be from a bank. They may say there is a problem with someone's account and ask them to share their private details. These messages are made to scare people into acting quickly. Real banks should not ask people to share their passwords through strange messages or links.


Fake Job Scams

Fake job scams happen when scammers advertise jobs that do not really exist. They may promise people a lot of money for very little work. They may ask people to pay a fee before getting the job. After receiving the money, they may disappear without giving the person any work.
