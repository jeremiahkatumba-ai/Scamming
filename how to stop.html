Protect Your Information

One way to stay safe from scammers is to protect your personal information. Never share your passwords, bank details, phone number or other private information with strangers online. Keep your passwords private and only share information with people you trust. This can help stop scammers from accessing your accounts or using your details without permission.


Check Before You Trust

Always check if a message, website or offer is real before trusting it. Some scammers create fake websites and messages that look like they belong to real companies. Do not believe every offer you see on the internet, especially if it sounds too good to be true. Take your time to check the information before making a decision.


Do Not Click Strange Links

Avoid clicking links from people or websites you do not know or trust. Some links may take you to fake websites that try to collect your personal information. Before clicking a link, check who sent it and whether the website looks trustworthy. If a message seems strange, do not open the link.


Ask for Help

If you think someone is trying to scam you, stop replying to the person and tell a trusted adult, parent or teacher about what happened. You can also report the account or message on the website or app where you received it. Asking for help can stop the problem from becoming worse.
